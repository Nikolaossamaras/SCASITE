About me

My path to my Dream

Hello, my name is Nikos Samaras, I live in Greece and at the age of 10 I started my coding journey. I started with the basics — learned Scratch, then HTML, Unity, and Python. At the age of 13 I discovered the realm of electronics and hardware. I learned basic electronics with Arduino and became interested in making all kinds of projects — hardware, software, crafts, and the list goes on.

I was really excited when I first heard of Hack Club. I first heard of Hack Club from a younger friend of mine that wanted to participate in clubs but couldn't because of his age. Until now I have learned Python, Godot, C++, JavaScript, CSS, HTML, and basic electronics. In the future I want to become an electrical or software engineer. My dream is to spend my life building cool stuff and hopefully be useful to the public.
